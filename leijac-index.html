About Leijac

Leijac Digital Entertainment is an independent game development studio based in Western North Carolina.

Founded in 2026 by Francisco Adrian Garcia, Leijac was built on a simple belief — that small teams with big vision can create experiences that stay with you.

We specialize in atmospheric, story-driven games that prioritize art direction, sound design, and world building above all else. Every project we touch is crafted with intention — from the shaders to the soundtrack.

"Art direction is paramount. Every pixel is deliberate. Every sound is intentional."

The name Leijac traces back to a Japanese arcade publisher founded in Osaka in 1975. They published games people loved. Then in September 1981, without any public announcement, they simply disappeared — absorbed into history by Konami Industries.

45 years later, the name is back. This time it's not going anywhere.

🎌

The Leijac Story

1975 — Leijac is founded in Osaka, Japan as a publisher of arcade games and medal games. A joint venture between Marusan Shōkai and Katō, two arcade operators.

1979 — Konami Industries begins quietly developing all of Leijac's games behind the scenes.

1981 — Konami takes full control. Leijac stops appearing in Game Machine magazine in September 1981. No announcement is ever made.

2026 — Leijac Digital Entertainment LLC is registered in North Carolina by Francisco Garcia. The name lives again.

The ghost developer took control of the publisher back then. This time, the developer IS the publisher.
